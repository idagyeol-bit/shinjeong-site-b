Services

Shinjeong Development removes residue, sediment and catalysts from inside industrial and environmental facilities, and cleans and dredges them. Our work falls into five services.

Five Services

Equipment Cleaning

We remove residue and sediment from inside tanks, ponds, drainage channels and R.T.O units.

  • Tanks
  • Ponds
  • Plant drainage channels
  • R.T.O
  • Filter Press
  • Bag Filter
Learn more

Catalyst & Packing Media

We remove and replace catalysts and packing media in reactors.

  • Reactor catalysts
  • Packing media
  • Adsorbents
  • Desiccants
Learn more

Chemical Cleaning

We clean piping, heat exchangers, cooling towers and boilers.

  • Piping
  • Heat exchangers
  • Cooling towers
  • Boiler tubes
Learn more

Dredging & Sludge Recovery

We dredge sediment from sewer and wastewater pipes and treatment facilities, and recover sludge.

  • Sewer pipes
  • Wastewater pipes
  • Sludge at sewage & wastewater treatment plants
Learn more

Pipeline & Underground Survey and Repair

We survey with pipeline CCTV and GPR, and repair without excavation.

  • Pipeline CCTV survey
  • GPR survey of buried utilities
  • Trenchless repair
