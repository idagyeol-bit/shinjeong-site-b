Robot Work Setup and Conditions

Work Setup

The robot works inside the equipment; the vacuum truck stays outside, connected by hose.

Work Setup
ComponentLocationRole
RobotInside the equipmentRecovers the remaining hazardous material and washes the interior with its front jet nozzle
Control vehicle (remote control · CCTV)Outside the equipmentThe operator controls the robot while watching the interior on CCTV
Vacuum truckOutside the equipmentConnected by hose; draws in the recovered material
SeparatorOutside the equipmentSeparates the recovered material in catalyst and packing media work

Conditions for Use

Whether to send in a robot, and how the work is done, is decided after checking the five points below and running a preliminary test.

  • 01Access openings & travel path

    Size of the openings the equipment passes through, and its travel path inside

  • 02Internal coils & obstacles

    Coils, structures and other obstacles inside the equipment

  • 03Residue properties

    State and characteristics of the residue

  • 04Temperature

    Temperature of the equipment interior and of the residue

  • 05Hose interference

    Places where the hose could catch or tangle as the equipment moves
