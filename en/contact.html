Frequently Asked Questions

      

      

        

          What information helps you advise us?
          
Tell us the target equipment, the purpose of the work, the site condition and your preferred schedule. Sending what you know — size, structure, photos, drawings — lets us give you a more accurate answer.

        

        

          Can you tell right away whether it applies to our equipment?
          
We can tell after checking site conditions such as the access openings, internal structure and residue characteristics. Send us that information and we will tell you what scope is applicable.

        

        

          Can we also ask about what happens to the recovered sludge?
          
Yes. You can also ask about dewatering, separation and the hand-over to treatment, depending on the recovered material and site conditions.

        

        

          Do robots do all of the work?
          
No. The robot first recovers the hazardous material inside the equipment; once the hazard is reduced, workers enter and remove the final residue. How far robots are used depends on the equipment and the working conditions.

        

        

          Why is it safer to send in a robot for confined-space work?
          
The robot removes hazardous material before anyone enters, while the operator controls it from a control vehicle outside the equipment, watching the interior on CCTV. This reduces the time people are exposed to risks such as suffocation by toxic gas, contact with high-risk substances and injury in tight spaces.

        

        

          Can it be used in explosion-proof zones or nitrogen-atmosphere equipment?
          
We operate equipment built for explosion-proof zones (hydraulic drive, explosion-proof camera, non-spark materials), and removing packing media inside reactors and tanks under a nitrogen atmosphere is within the scope.
